📋 WhatsApp Templates — Meta Business Manager Approval Guide

All templates below must be submitted in Meta Business Manager → WhatsApp Manager → Message Templates. Templates in UTILITY category have the highest approval rate and lowest per-message cost. Once approved, send_template_or_text() in the codebase uses the template automatically.

⚠️ Submission tips:
  • Language: English (en)
  • Parameters use {{1}}, {{2}}, … syntax in the Meta UI
  • Keep body under 1024 characters
  • Add sample values for each parameter (required by Meta for review)
  • Approval typically takes 1–24 hours
  • While pending: send_template_or_text() falls back to plain text — nothing breaks during the window
